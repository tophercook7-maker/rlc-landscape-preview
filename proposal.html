The Yelp side — keep what's working

Yelp's already bringing you real leads, so the plan is to make it work harder, not replace it:

  • The disappearing reviews are normal — Yelp temporarily hides new reviews while it checks the reviewer's account; they typically return within 60–90 days. Not something broken on your end.
  • Reviews stick better when the reviewer's own profile is filled out (photo, a few details) — worth a quick mention when you ask.
  • Space review requests out — a few a week reads as organic; a dozen in one week can trip Yelp's filter even when every review is real.
  • Consider the Verified License badge (~$1/day) — landscaping qualifies, and Yelp's own data shows about a 10% average lift in calls/clicks/map views after adding it.
